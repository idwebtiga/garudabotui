# Garuda BOT — Landing Page Plan

## 1. Strategic Overview

**Primary Goal:** Get visitors to sign in and start chatting with Garuda BOT.

**Target Audience:**
- Indonesian community members interested in AI
- People who want AI that reflects their culture and values
- Those interested in community-governed technology

**Core Value Proposition (one sentence):**
> *Garuda BOT is the first AI shaped by its Indonesian community — your tokens, your voice.*

**Tone:** Bold, culturally proud, community-first. Never corporate or salesy.

---

## 2. Page Sections (in order)

### Section 1 — Hero
**Goal:** Capture attention and state the proposition in under 5 seconds.

- **Headline:** `AI yang Lahir dari Komunitas` *(AI Born from the Community)*
- **Subheadline:** Garuda BOT is an AI assistant shaped by its community — built for Indonesia, governed by Indonesians.
- **Primary CTA:** `Sign In & Start Chatting`
- **Secondary CTA:** `Try Without Signing In`
- **Credibility hint:** Small note — "Free to use · Community governed"

---

### Section 2 — The Problem (Why This Exists)
**Goal:** Create emotional resonance with the Indonesian audience.

- **Headline:** *"AI systems today don't speak our language — not really."*
- 2–3 short statements (icon + text):
  - Existing AI is built by and for Western cultural contexts
  - Indonesian values, idioms, and priorities are treated as afterthoughts
  - Communities have no say in how AI behaves
- **Transition line:** "Garuda BOT changes that."

---

### Section 3 — How It Works (3-Step Flow)
**Goal:** Make the concept immediately understandable.

Three steps shown visually:

1. **Chat Freely** — Talk to Garuda BOT about topics that matter to you. No sign-in required to get started.
2. **Sign In to Influence** — Connect your account to link your GARUDA token holdings and gain a weighted voice.
3. **Shape the AI** — The more you hold, the more your conversations influence how the AI evolves over time.

---

### Section 4 — Influence Tiers
**Goal:** Show the incentive structure clearly and encourage token holding.

- **Headline:** *Your Voice, Your Tier*
- **Supporting copy:** The more GARUDA tokens you hold, the greater your influence on the AI's direction.

| Tier | Stars | Influence Multiplier | Token Range |
|------|-------|----------------------|-------------|
| 1 | ★ | ×1 | 1 – 1,000 |
| 2 | ★★ | ×2 | 1,001 – 10,000 |

| 4 | ★★★★ | ×4 | 100,001 – 500,000 |
| 5 | ★★★★★ | ×5 | 500,001 – 1,000,000 |

- Tier 0 note: "No tokens? No problem. You can still chat freely."
- **CTA below tiers:** `Get GARUDA Tokens →`

---

### Section 5 — Key Features
**Goal:** Reinforce what makes Garuda BOT different.

Six features as a grid (icon + title + short description):

1. **Community-Shaped AI** — Every conversation from token holders contributes to how the AI grows and responds.
2. **Indonesian at Heart** — Built around Indonesian language, culture, current affairs, and community values.
3. **No Central Authority** — No single person or company controls Garuda BOT's direction. The community does.
4. **Open to Everyone** — Anyone can chat, sign in or not. Influence grows with participation.
5. **Evolving Personality** — Watch the AI's collective character develop over time through a live dashboard.
6. **Transparent by Design** — Token holdings and influence weights are publicly verifiable at any time.

**Goal:** Build emotional loyalty with mission-driven visitors.

Full-width centered quote:

## 3. Copywriting Notes

- **Primary language:** Bilingual — Bahasa Indonesia for headlines, English for body text. Mirrors how the audience naturally communicates.
- **Key phrases:** *komunitas*, *kedaulatan*, *suara rakyat*, *bersama kita bentuk*
- **Always emphasize:** Chat is free and open. Sign-in unlocks influence, not access.
- The sign-in flow should feel like joining a community, not a transaction.

---

## 4. Conversion Notes

- The primary CTA ("Sign In & Start Chatting") should appear in the Hero and again in the Final CTA section.
- The "Try Without Signing In" secondary CTA reduces friction for skeptical first-time visitors — getting them into the chat experience is more valuable than losing them at the gate.
- The tier section should feel aspirational, not gatekeeping.
- Avoid navigation links that pull visitors away from the page before they act.
